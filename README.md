# Year 12–13 IB Diploma English A: Literature (SL & HL)

Interactive revision dashboard, transferable literary analysis studio, Individual Oral & HL Essay workshop, and unseen exam bank for **IB Diploma Language A: Literature (First Assessment 2021)** — built for Vanishree (Tanglin Trust School, Singapore).

## Non-Prescriptive Text Policy
Zero set texts are assumed. Every topic teaches transferable literary craft across Poetry, Drama, Prose Fiction, and Literary Non-Fiction; all illustrative named works carry an explicit **`[Example text — not a set text]`** tag so every framework transfers directly onto your own studied works.

## Structure
- **8 Topics** covering the 3 Areas of Exploration, all 4 Literary Forms, the 7 Concepts & 5 Fields of Inquiry, Paper 1 & Paper 2 Exam Craft, and the HL Essay (`level: "HL"`).
- **4 Mock Papers:** Diagnostic MCQs (25 Qs), Paper 1 SL (15 Qs), Paper 1 HL (15 Qs, 2 unseen passages), and Paper 2 Comparative Essay (15 Qs).
- **Individual Oral & HL Essay Guide (`/ia`):** Global Issue 3-property check, 10-bullet outline & 10-minute pacing plan, Criterion A–D rubrics, and non-overlap work rules.
- **Lit Toolkit (`/formulas`), 60s Fluency Sprint (`/sprint`), Daily 5 (`/daily`), and Interactive Explorables** across all 8 topics.
